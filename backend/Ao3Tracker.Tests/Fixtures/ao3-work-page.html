"I miss the old times when you used to love me," Zoey sniffed, crocodile tears barely coming. She knew complaining was pointless; Mira always got so bossy when they were rehearsing choreography.

Rehearsals for their new comeback have been chaotic since they started. After enjoying a two-week break in Jeju Island, getting back into their normal routine was proving a bit difficult.

They usually felt refreshed after a two-week break between a world tour and their next comeback, but this time they still felt somewhat exhausted.

Perhaps this was because, even on a break from work, they kept working. In the three weeks since they returned to Seoul, the new album already has songs ready because Zoey, even on vacation, can't stop ideas from emerging. Lyrics and melodies found their way into her notebooks whenever inspiration struck her.

She's their leader, after all, and she needs to show determination, to set an example.

It was true that she was tired, but feeling dizzy was something new. It had never happened before, no matter how difficult the choreography.

Everything was perfectly fine, just tiredness and thirst.

Maybe it was just a drop in blood pressure. Maybe she was just too tired; the choreography was just more complicated that time. She's fine. It's been two days since the fainting, and everything is perfectly normal. Nothing to worry about.
